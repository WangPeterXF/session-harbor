# Third-party projects

No third-party source code is vendored in version 0.3.0.

The optional compression workflow invokes [`@milisp/codex-slim`](https://github.com/milisp/codex-slim), an independently installed MIT-licensed project. SessionHarbor does not bundle its binary or claim authorship of its compression implementation.

Architecture and safety research also reviewed these independent MIT-licensed projects:

- [`codex-project-chat-exporter`](https://github.com/Ann-Diana/codex-project-chat-exporter)
- [`coldxx`](https://github.com/outx-sec/coldxx)
- [`codex-session-sync`](https://github.com/shonngithub/codex-session-sync)
- [`sessionark`](https://github.com/julescules/sessionark)
- [`codex-export`](https://github.com/ezyyeah/codex-export)

Re-check upstream licenses before copying code or adding dependencies.

Those third-party projects retain their own licenses. SessionHarbor's PolyForm Noncommercial
license does not relicense or impose SessionHarbor terms on independently installed third-party
software.
